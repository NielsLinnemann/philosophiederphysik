3. Wochenendseminar (Köln/Online)

Das dritte Seminar fand erneut online statt. Schwerpunkt war die Philosophie der Zeit. Vier renommierte Gastvortragende beleuchteten das Thema aus unterschiedlichen Perspektiven, von analytischer Metaphysik bis zur Allgemeinen Relativitätstheorie.

Vortragende

Jenann Ismael
Columbia University
Time and the Visual Imagination

The visual imagination plays an especially prominent role in spacetime physics. Spacetime diagrams and other visualizations guide the imagination in computation. The speaker discusses the images of time coming out of physics and the philosophical confusions to which they give rise.

Natalja Deng
Yonsei University
Die Probleme der Zeitphilosophie
Lucy James
University of Bristol
Relational Observables and Time

In Einstein's theory of General Relativity, ambiguities arise as to the physicality of time. The talk introduces gauge invariance and describes the general form of a gauge invariant observable according to Rovelli's formulation of classical mechanics.

Andreas Hüttemann
Universität Köln
Wie wäre es, wenn es keine Zeit gäbe?
