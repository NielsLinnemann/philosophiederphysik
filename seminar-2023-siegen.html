Beschreibung

5. Seminar (Siegen)

Schwerpunktthemen waren Ununterscheidbarkeit und Leibniz-Prinzip, Quantenlogik sowie Interpretation der Quantenmechanik. Neben den Fachvorträgen hielten Studierende eigene Präsentationen.

Vortragende

Veronika Baumann
Universität Wien
Wigner's friend type experiments and the interpretations of quantum theory

Wigner's-friend experiments exploit the fact that quantum theory comprises two different dynamics: the collapse of the wave function and unitary evolution. New proposals involving Wigner's friend experiments have sparked renewed debate in the quantum-foundations community.

Tomasz Bigaj
Universität Warschau
On the discernibility of same-type particles

A formal method of discerning quantum particles consistent with the symmetrization postulate. In the case of fermions the method guarantees absolute discernibility in any state, whereas for bosons the indiscernibility remains for products of identical states.

Sonja Smets
Universiteit van Amsterdam
Logic Meets Quantum Physics

Concepts from modal logic and dynamic logic can be used to model and interpret quantum behavior. The main thesis: all non-classical properties of quantum systems are explainable in terms of the non-classical flow of quantum information.

Cord Friebe
Universität Siegen
